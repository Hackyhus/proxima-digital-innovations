PRODUCTS & PLATFORMS

Mission-Critical Systems Engineered for Demanding Operations

Four specialized enterprise platforms built to solve operational bottlenecks, eliminate cash leakage, automate complex records, and enforce strict identity governance.

PROXIMA-BMS ERP Platform

Proxima BMS / ERP

Business Management & Enterprise Resource Planning

Unified operational control for commercial enterprises, wholesale distributors, retail chains, and manufacturing facilities. Eliminates inventory discrepancies, automates double-entry accounting, and connects multi-location depots into a single synchronized ledger.

  • Multi-branch inventory reconciliation & warehouse transfers
  • Automated general ledger, accounts receivable/payable, and POS
  • Offline transaction journaling: register continues if internet fails
  • Immutable audit logs and granular role-based permissions
PROXIMA-HMS Clinical Platform

Proxima HMS

Hospital & Clinical Management System

High-reliability clinical software for private hospitals, specialized surgical centers, and diagnostic laboratories. Designed to keep wards, pharmacies, and emergency desks operational without reliance on continuous cloud connectivity.

  • Electronic Medical Records (EMR) with strict patient confidentiality
  • Integrated pharmacy dispensing with batch expiry monitoring
  • Laboratory Information System (LIS) with barcode sample tracking
  • Automated NHIS / HMO billing reconciliation and debt prevention
PROXIMA-SMS Academic Platform

Proxima SMS

School & Institutional Management System

Comprehensive institutional management platform for primary schools, secondary academies, and tertiary colleges. Streamlines complex continuous assessments, grading computations, fee collections, and automated guardian reporting.

  • Automated continuous assessment (CA) & tamper-proof report cards
  • Fee invoice generation, bank reconciliation & parent receipts
  • Faculty workload allocation, timetabling & subject assignment
  • Biometric student clock-in integration for automated roll call
PROXIMA-BIO Hardware-Edge Security

Biometric Attendance & Security

Hardware-Integrated Time & Physical Access Control

Direct hardware-level drivers interfacing optical fingerprint scanners, facial verification terminals, and turnstile controllers. Eliminates buddy-punching, logs real-time entries with microsecond timestamps, and synchronizes directly with payroll ledgers.

  • Native drivers for Hikvision, ZKTeco, and proprietary edge controllers
  • Sub-300ms verification directly on hardware edge without cloud delay
  • Automated shift scheduling, overtime rules, and direct payroll export
  • Physical door relay interlocks with anti-passback and tamper sensors
